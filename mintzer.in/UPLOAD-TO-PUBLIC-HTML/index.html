FAQ

Questions about using the app.

What is Mintzer?

Mintzer lists store deals you can complete with your card. Accept a deal, place the order to the address on the deal, submit proof in the app, and get paid to your bank.

How do I earn?

Open a deal, accept it, and order on Flipkart or Amazon with the card shown. After you submit proof in the app and we verify the order, your earn is paid to your bank.

Whose address do I ship to?

Ship to the delivery address shown on the deal in the app. Use that address exactly at store checkout — not your own.

When do I get paid?

Within 48 working hours after your invoice is verified. Bank holidays are skipped; if the window ends on a holiday, payout moves to the next working day.

What does Wallet show?

Your earn history — how much has already been paid to your bank, and what’s still processing. Payouts go to your bank account, not as cash you pull from the app.

What do I need to start?

Just your phone number to log in. When you accept your first deal, you’ll add PAN and bank details so we can pay you. That’s it.

Where can I download the app?

On the App Store for iPhone and Google Play for Android.
